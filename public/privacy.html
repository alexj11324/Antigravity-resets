Email alerts

Subscribing is optional and uses double opt-in: after you submit an address, you receive one confirmation email and nothing is sent until you click the link in it. For each subscription the database stores the email address, the page language you chose, whether it is confirmed, and the timestamps of the request and the confirmation. That is the entire record. It is used only to send reset alerts.

Every alert email contains a one-click unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing deletes the record; no shadow copy is kept. The address is not sold, rented, or shared with sponsors, and it is never used for anything except reset notifications.

What is never collected

The tracker does not ask for and cannot access your Google account, your Antigravity session, your IDE, or your personal quota meter. There is no advertising pixel, no cross-site tracker, and no third-party cookie. No account is required to use any part of the site.

Local storage and the presence counter

Your browser stores two preferences locally: the light/dark theme and the timezone mode. They never leave your device. The "here now" counter sends a random session identifier and a timestamp; the identifier is not linked to an IP address, a device fingerprint, or an email, and it is deleted automatically after a short time.

Hosting, logs, and email delivery

The site runs on Cloudflare Workers with a Cloudflare D1 database, and alert email is delivered through a transactional email provider. Those providers process requests and deliver mail on our behalf and have their own privacy terms. Aggregate traffic analytics are cookieless.

Your choices

Unsubscribe from any alert email, or ask for removal by writing in the alert channel: t.me/antigravity_resets_alert. See also about this project.
