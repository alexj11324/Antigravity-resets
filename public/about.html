What this site is

Antigravity Resets is an independent, non-commercial community tracker. It watches the public X accounts that announce Google Antigravity quota resets, keeps a verified history, and publishes it through a free, keyless API. It is not affiliated with Google DeepMind, Google Antigravity, or the people it tracks.

How announcements are verified

A post is accepted only when an official account announces a broad reset that is live or explicitly scheduled. Teaser posts, polls, retrospectives, quota increases without a reset, routine explanations of the five-hour window, and one-to-one support replies are rejected. Each candidate is classified twice by two different models; disagreements go to a third model and then to a human. Every published reset links to the original post so you can check it yourself.

What the tracker reads — and what it never touches

The tracker reads public X posts and their public metadata. It never asks for, stores, or uses your Google account, your Antigravity session, or your personal quota meter. It cannot tell you your remaining quota; it can only tell you what was officially announced.

How it is funded

Hosting runs on Cloudflare's free and low-cost tiers. Optional sponsor placements are clearly labelled and are the only commercial content on the site. Sponsors do not see subscriber data and have no say in what the tracker publishes.
